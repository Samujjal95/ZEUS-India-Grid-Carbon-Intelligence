What ZEUS provides

Three things missing from the static-baseline status quo.

Until now, defensible Indian grid intensity numbers came from annual-average baselines. ZEUS adds 15-minute resolution, documented uncertainty, and forecast accuracy on top of CEA's plant-level factors.

15-min historical intensity

Every 15-minute block since April 2025, sourced directly from GRID-INDIA Power Supply Position TimeSeries. CEA-anchored emission factors (v21.0 plant-level factors applied to live thermal mix). Period-correct means for FY 2025-26, FY 2026-27 YTD, rolling 12 months, or full dataset.

48-hour forecast

XGBoost + Optuna + conformal prediction across 5 horizons (1h, 6h, 12h, 24h, 48h). 2.84–3.87% MAPE on monsoon 2025 holdout — model never saw Jul–Sep 2025 data, including the late-September Bay of Bengal cyclone formation.

Audit-defensible methodology

Full lineage trail. ±5% uncertainty disclosed (compatible with ISO 14064-1 organizational reporting). Every factor citable with v21.0 plant-level reference. Built for top 250 listed entities subject to BRSR Core reasonable assurance — your auditor can drill into any 15-minute block.
