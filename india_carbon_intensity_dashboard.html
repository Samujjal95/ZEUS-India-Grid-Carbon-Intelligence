In plain terms: Every time you use electricity in India, some CO₂ is emitted to generate it. The mix of coal, gas, hydro, wind, and solar changes every 15 minutes — so the carbon footprint changes too. This product measures that footprint in real time, so you can report it accurately and reduce it practically.

3. Formula
// Per 15-min block t: intensity(t) = Σ[gen_fuel(t) × EF_fuel] ——————————— total_generation(t) // × 1000 → g CO₂/kWh
In plain terms: For each 15-min period, multiply each fuel’s output (MW) by its CO₂ factor, divide by total generation. Result = grams of CO₂ per kWh consumed.
4. Scope & Assumptions
In scope: All-India average · Location-based Scope 2 · GHG Protocol · BRSR · CCTS · 15-min granularity · State-level intensity (8 states) ✓ AVAILABLE NOW

Assumptions: THERMAL = 95% coal + 5% lignite · Imports = zero (CEA Tool v7.0 §10) · BESS <1 GW, not modelled · Misc. generation = 0.70 tCO₂/MWh

Not yet: Marginal intensity · Forecast · Market-based Scope 2

Why the difference? CEA’s 712 includes the monsoon season (Jul–Sep) when intensity drops sharply. Our data starts April 2025 — summer months when thermal load peaks. Once our dataset covers a full year including monsoon, the mean will align closely. The gap is expected and documented, not an error.
